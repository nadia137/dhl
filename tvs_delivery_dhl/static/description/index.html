DHL Express Shipping

Send your packages safely and efficiently through DHL — one of the world’s most trusted courier services. Enjoy fast, reliable, and secure delivery for all your shipments, whether local or international. You can easily track your parcels online in real-time, receive instant updates, and stay informed about every step of your delivery journey from dispatch to destination

Key Features

Direct Label Generation

Generate official DHL shipping labels and commercial invoices directly from the Odoo delivery order with a single click. No more re-typing addresses or package details.

Real-Time Rate Calculation

Automatically fetch and display live DHL shipping rates on your Sales Orders or at the time of delivery, allowing you to accurately charge customers or compare service costs.

Automatic Tracking Number Retrieval

Once a label is created, the official DHL tracking number is automatically fetched and stored in Odoo, linking it directly to the delivery order.

Automated Customer Notifications

The module seamlessly integrates with Odoo's notification system. Your customers will automatically receive an email with their tracking number and a link to the DHL tracking site as soon as the order is shipped.

Support for All Shipment Types

Whether you're sending a document, a small parcel, or a large international shipment, the module supports all major DHL services and handles both domestic and international logistics.

Effortless Return Label Generation

Manage customer returns with ease. The module allows you to generate DHL return labels just as easily as outbound ones, simplifying your reverse logistics process.
